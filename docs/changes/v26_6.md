# v0.26.6: shared-grid mesh study and revised acceptance

The user selected `neff_kappa_only_v1` on November 27, 2026. Mesh/domain and
endpoint effective-index comparisons use `1e-4`. Detuning differences and their
length-times-error estimates remain visible, but do not determine mesh/domain
acceptance. Complex coupling keeps its existing absolute-plus-relative and
accumulated-phase error gates; loss, field, polarization, tracking, extraction
validity and handoff arithmetic/sign checks remain active. Blackman detuning
fitting and longitudinal/table interpolation validation remain separate.

The named diagnostic `endpoint_shared_grid_mesh_study_v2_neff_kappa_only` keeps
both mesh regions and the interlayer optical region in upper-reference,
lower-reference and full-coupler solves, including when a ridge is removed.
Removed-ridge dimensions remain in raw cache identities. Actual mesh coordinates
are archived and checked rather than inferred from requested counts.

Independent x/y ladders require two consecutive passing comparisons. Combined
mesh checks cover both states at 1260/1340 nm, with input, zero-offset center,
3 um shoulder and output sections. Conditional domain, PML and joint guards
follow. The fixed center is not assumed to be phase matched. Frozen geometry,
optical settings and coupling branch conventions are preserved.

The matched-slab reference remains a diagnostic alternative. This study neither
changes production reference physics nor certifies the validity of an orthogonal
two-mode basis. It does not issue a Block 0 certificate or automatically launch
a production optimization. Numerical convergence and physical device validation
remain distinct.

The first study was stopped at the user's request after 77 completed FDE calls;
its saved comparisons retain the old `2e-5` and detuning criteria. The revised
study uses a fresh output directory and explicit budgets, with its own source
identity. No earlier evidence is relabeled as convergence under the new policy.
Changed source/accuracy identities invalidate previous calibration certificates.

See [targeted mesh study](../methods/targeted-mesh-study.md) for invocation,
budgets, checkpoints and output descriptions.
