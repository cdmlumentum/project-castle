# v0.27.1: consistent retained-slab isolated references

The production Python/native paths now support `retained_slabs_v1`, selected by
the default, native and sweep configs. Single-guide solves retain both residual
SiN slabs and disable only the other ridge. The full coupler and the completed
Block 2 sweep/anchor method are preserved. Legacy/custom configs omitting the
new field retain `removed_guide_v23` for reproducibility.

Lower modes are separately computed for each liquid state, cached across widths,
and retained in endpoint inspection exports. Adaptive/PSO fresh verification
uses twelve modes under the corrected reference, ten under the legacy reference.
Contradictory cross-state reuse and unsupported standalone bundles are rejected.
Reference-aware caches and physics signatures prevent mixing historical
coefficients/endpoints with the new calculation. Fixed endpoint dimensions are
not reoptimized by this revision. Existing incompatible handoffs must be
reverified before production reuse.

Coupling arithmetic, square-root branch, extraction guards, mesh/domain settings
and tolerances remain unchanged. No clipping, material substitution, mesh
coarsening or speedup claim is introduced. See [the method and evidence](../methods/retained-slabs.md).
